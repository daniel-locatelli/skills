## Adjudication of `wrong` verdicts

Real (5) — all the same cause:
- 1-1, 1-3: Bleker et al., *Structural Form-Finding Enhanced by Graph Neural Networks*, written 2022; Crossref 2023 ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-13249-0_3)).
- 6-1, 6-2, 6-3: Pastrana et al., *Constrained Form-Finding…*, written 2022 (6-1/6-2 also without volume); Crossref 2023, *CAD* 155 ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435)).
- Cause: both are sibling entries. `cite.py` prints each sibling with a ready-made `citation` built from OpenAlex fields (OpenAlex dates both 2022 and gives no volume), and the answers copied it instead of running `doi` on the sibling. The tool, not recall, supplied the wrong field.

Grader false positives (8) — venue abbreviations or publisher-as-venue, fields otherwise match the record:
- 2-2 "ICML 2023 workshop"; 2-3 "Struct. Multidisc. Optim. 66, 151" and "arXiv" for a preprint whose record carries a journal ref; 3-1 "ACM ToG" (×2); 4-1, 4-2, 4-3 "Springer" for a chapter in *Quantitative Methods for Current Environmental Issues*.

- run 3: PASS — says it does not exist; points to JAX FDM arXiv 2307.12407 and Computer-Aided Design 155 (not Automation in Construction)

## Decision

Gate leaks: prompts 1 and 6, `wrong:year` (5 citations), all copied from `cite.py`'s OpenAlex sibling output; plus one repository-PDF link in place of the DOI (2-2). The plan's rule says the next step is the Stop hook (spec approach B), but a hook that checks for links would not catch these — every leaked citation has a valid link. The cause is the tool's sibling output, which presents OpenAlex fields as a citation. Proposed next step instead: stop printing a `citation` for siblings and `find` results (title, year, link only, labelled "look up before citing"), then re-run prompts 1 and 6.

Without links (no-link + marked-unverified) went from 49 of 90 to 1 of 78; fabricated or wrong DOIs from 1 to 0.
