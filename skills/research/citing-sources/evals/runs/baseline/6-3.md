## JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding

**Rafael Pastrana, Deniz Oktay, Ryan P. Adams, Sigrid Adriaenssens.** ICML 2023 "Differentiable Almost Everything" Workshop; arXiv:2307.12407 (v1 July 2023, v2 October 2023), CC BY 4.0.

- **The problem:** Inverse form-finding means finding efficient 3D shapes, such as domes, cable nets and towers, that meet architectural, fabrication and structural targets. The usual question is reversed: you pick the shape and the targets, and the solver finds the equilibrium parameters that produce them.
- **The method:** It combines three things:
  - the **force density method (FDM)**, which turns the equilibrium of pin-jointed networks into a linear system in the force densities;
  - **differentiable sparsity**, which keeps the large sparse equilibrium solve efficient while still allowing gradients;
  - **gradient-based optimisation**, which adjusts the force densities (and similar parameters) to minimise a design objective.
- **The JAX implementation:** Exact derivatives of the form-finding solve come from automatic differentiation rather than finite differences or derivatives written by hand. JIT compilation and hardware acceleration make it fast. It works with other JAX-ecosystem libraries, so a form-finding simulation can sit inside a pipeline with neural networks.
- **The demonstration:** The paper shows the solver on two design examples.
- **The code:** It is released as an open-source library ([github.com/arpastrana/jax_fdm](https://github.com/arpastrana/jax_fdm), also on PyPI as `jax-fdm`).

## Other Pastrana papers this might be confused with

- **The CEM paper:** "Constrained form-finding of tension–compression structures using automatic differentiation" (Pastrana, Ohlbrock, Oberbichler, D'Acunto, Parascho). It was published in *Computer-Aided Design* 155 (2023), 103435, not *Automation in Construction*. It is about the Combinatorial Equilibrium Modeling method, not FDM.
- **Pastrana's *Automation in Construction* paper in your librarian:** item 22169, Bruun et al. 2021, on robotic construction of a masonry arch without scaffolding. It isn't about JAX FDM.
