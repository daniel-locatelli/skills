Rafael Pastrana has four papers that are directly about form-finding with automatic differentiation, and a fifth that builds on that work. All of them are by the same author, R. Pastrana (OpenAlex [A5073512746](https://openalex.org/A5073512746)). I took every field below from the Crossref or arXiv record.

**Directly on form-finding with automatic differentiation**

1. **Constrained form-finding with AD (CEM).** Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O., Oberbichler, T., D'Acunto, P. & Parascho, S. (2023). *Constrained Form-Finding of Tension–Compression Structures using Automatic Differentiation.* Computer-Aided Design 155, 103435. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435
   - This makes the Combinatorial Equilibrium Modeling (CEM) form-finding method differentiable, so that constraints can be met by gradient-based optimisation.
   - OpenAlex lists it as 2022 (the online-first date). The journal volume is dated 2023, so cite it as 2023.

2. **JAX FDM.** Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* ICML 2023 Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407
   - This is a differentiable force density method (FDM) solver written in JAX, used for inverse form-finding.
   - It was a workshop paper. It is not a journal article, so don't cite it as one.

3. **Differentiable FDM, journal paper.** Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Bletzinger, K. U., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2026). *Differentiable force density method for the design of lightweight structures.* Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering 458, 118783. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2026.118783
   - This is the full journal paper on the differentiable FDM. It is a separate publication from the 2023 JAX FDM workshop paper, so keep the two apart when citing.

4. **Differentiable mechanics combined with neural networks.** Pastrana, R., Medina, E., Oliveira, I. M. d., Adriaenssens, S. & Adams, R. P. (2024). *Real-time design of architectural structures with differentiable mechanics and neural networks.* arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02606
   - A neural network is trained with a differentiable form-finding simulator in the loop, so that structures can be designed in real time.
   - I couldn't find a peer-reviewed version in OpenAlex, so cite it as a preprint.

**Related work**

- Oval, R., Pastrana, R., et al. (2024). *An integrated digital design and construction approach for falsework-minimal masonry vaults.* Structures 63, 106428. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.istruc.2024.106428. This applies the approach to masonry vaults. Pastrana is second author.
- Bleker, L., Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O. & D'Acunto, P. (2023). *Structural Form-Finding Enhanced by Graph Neural Networks.* In *Towards Radical Regeneration*, 24–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-13249-0_3. This one uses graph neural networks for form-finding, not automatic differentiation. Pastrana is a co-author.
