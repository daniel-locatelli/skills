Rafael Pastrana has four papers on form-finding with automatic differentiation, plus one closely related paper. I looked up each one and took the details from Crossref and arXiv.

**Core papers**

1. Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O., Oberbichler, T., D'Acunto, P. & Parascho, S. (2023). *Constrained Form-Finding of Tension–Compression Structures using Automatic Differentiation.* Computer-Aided Design 155, 103435. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435
   This is the first one. It uses automatic differentiation for constrained form-finding of tension–compression structures. OpenAlex lists it as 2022, which is probably the online-first date; the journal volume is 2023.

2. Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* ICML 2023, Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407
   A force density method solver written in JAX, used for inverse form-finding. It's a workshop paper, not a journal article. Don't mix it up with the Computer-Aided Design paper from the same year.

3. Pastrana, R., Medina, E., Oliveira, I. M. d., Adriaenssens, S. & Adams, R. P. (2024). *Real-time design of architectural structures with differentiable mechanics and neural networks.* arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02606
   Combines neural networks with a differentiable mechanics simulator so structures can be designed in real time. The record only lists the arXiv preprint. I didn't confirm whether it has since been published at a venue.

4. Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Bletzinger, K. U., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2026). *Differentiable force density method for the design of lightweight structures.* Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering 458, 118783. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2026.118783
   The most recent: a full journal treatment of the differentiable force density method.

**Related, but not automatic differentiation itself**

- Bleker, L., Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O. & D'Acunto, P. (2023). *Structural Form-Finding Enhanced by Graph Neural Networks.* In *Towards Radical Regeneration*, 24–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-13249-0_3. This one uses machine learning for form-finding, and Pastrana is second author.

OpenAlex shows his other recent papers as construction work on masonry and thin-tile vaults, not differentiable form-finding. His open-source `jax_fdm` library goes with paper 2.
