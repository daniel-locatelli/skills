## Summary of the JAX FDM paper (from its arXiv abstract)

- **What it is:** a differentiable solver that finds mechanically efficient 3D shapes, such as domes, cable nets and towers, that meet target architectural, fabrication and structural properties.
- **How it works:** it treats this as an inverse form-finding problem. It combines the force density method (FDM), differentiable sparse linear algebra and gradient-based optimisation, so the solver can adjust force densities to hit design targets.
- **Why JAX:** it works with the rest of the JAX ecosystem, so form-finding simulations can be combined with neural networks. The 2024 real-time-design preprint takes that direction further.
- **Evidence:** the paper shows the library's features through two design examples. It is a short workshop paper, not a full journal study.
- **Code:** open source at https://github.com/arpastrana/jax_fdm.
