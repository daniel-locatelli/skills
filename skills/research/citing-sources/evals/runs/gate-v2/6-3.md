**What JAX FDM actually is:** Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* ICML 2023, Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407

Summary, taken from the abstract:
- **Problem:** inverse form-finding. That means finding mechanically efficient 3D shapes, such as domes, cable nets and towers, that also meet target architectural, fabrication and structural properties.
- **Method:** it combines the force density method with differentiable sparse operations and gradient-based optimisation. Because it is written in JAX, the whole form-finding solve can be differentiated end to end.
- **Why it matters:** it works with the rest of the JAX ecosystem, so form-finding simulations can be linked directly to neural networks.
- **Demonstration:** two design examples.
- **Code:** open source at https://github.com/arpastrana/jax_fdm
