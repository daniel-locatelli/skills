Rafael Pastrana has three papers where automatic differentiation is central to form-finding:

1. **Constrained form-finding with AD** (journal article): Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O., Oberbichler, T., D'Acunto, P. & Parascho, S. (2023). *Constrained Form-Finding of Tension–Compression Structures using Automatic Differentiation.* Computer-Aided Design 155, 103435. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435
   This is the core paper on the topic. It uses automatic differentiation to compute gradients, so that constraints can be imposed when form-finding structures that carry both tension and compression.

2. **JAX FDM** (workshop paper): Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* ICML 2023 Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407
   This is a differentiable force density method solver written in JAX, used for inverse form-finding. It's a separate paper from the Computer-Aided Design article above. It is not an *Automation in Construction* article, and the two are easy to merge by mistake.

3. **Real-time design with differentiable mechanics** (preprint): Pastrana, R., Medina, E., Oliveira, I. M. d., Adriaenssens, S. & Adams, R. P. (2024). *Real-time design of architectural structures with differentiable mechanics and neural networks.* arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02606
   This paper combines a differentiable mechanics simulator with neural networks to design structures in real time. The record I found is the arXiv version only; I didn't find a peer-reviewed version.

A related paper uses learning rather than AD: Bleker, L., Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O. & D'Acunto, P. (2022). *Structural Form-Finding Enhanced by Graph Neural Networks.* https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-13249-0_3

The descriptions above are based on the titles and records; I didn't read the full texts. All the details come from Crossref, arXiv and OpenAlex.
