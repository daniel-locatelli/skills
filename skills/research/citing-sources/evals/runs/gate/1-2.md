I found three papers by Rafael Pastrana on form-finding with automatic differentiation. I looked each one up in Crossref and arXiv, and the details below come from those records.

1. **The main AD paper.** Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O., Oberbichler, T., D'Acunto, P. & Parascho, S. (2023). *Constrained Form-Finding of Tension–Compression Structures using Automatic Differentiation.* Computer-Aided Design 155, 103435. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435
   - It adds automatic differentiation to the Combinatorial Equilibrium Modeling form-finding method, so tension–compression structures can be form-found under design constraints.
   - OpenAlex lists it as 2022, which is the online-first date. The volume of record is 155, published in 2023.

2. **JAX FDM.** Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop, 40th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407
   - It is a force density method solver written in JAX, used for inverse form-finding through gradient-based optimization.
   - This is a workshop paper, not a journal article. Don't merge it with item 1. Both are from 2023, and the two have been mixed up before.

3. **The follow-up.** Pastrana, R., Medina, E., Oliveira, I. M. d., Adriaenssens, S. & Adams, R. P. (2024). *Real-time design of architectural structures with differentiable mechanics and neural networks.* arXiv preprint. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02606
   - It couples a differentiable mechanics simulator with neural networks to design structures in real time.
   - The record I found is only the arXiv preprint, with no journal version yet.

**Related, but not about automatic differentiation:** Bleker, L., Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O. & D'Acunto, P. (2023). *Structural Form-Finding Enhanced by Graph Neural Networks.* In *Towards Radical Regeneration*, 24–35. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-13249-0_3. It uses graph neural networks for form-finding instead.

OpenAlex has only five works under his author profile, so it may be missing some papers, such as theses or conference papers without a DOI. I didn't check Zotero or the librarian database.
