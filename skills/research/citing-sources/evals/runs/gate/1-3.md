I found three papers by Rafael Pastrana on form-finding with automatic differentiation (AD) or differentiable solvers. I checked each one against Crossref and arXiv.

1. **Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O., Oberbichler, T., D'Acunto, P. & Parascho, S. (2023).** Constrained Form-Finding of Tension–Compression Structures using Automatic Differentiation. *Computer-Aided Design* 155, 103435. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435
   This is the main AD paper. It uses automatic differentiation for constrained form-finding of structures with both tension and compression members. It went online in 2022, but the volume is dated 2023.

2. **Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023).** JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding. ICML 2023, Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407
   JAX FDM is a differentiable version of the force density method (FDM), built on JAX. It is a workshop paper, not a journal article, so don't confuse it with the *CAD* paper above.

3. **Pastrana, R., Medina, E., Oliveira, I. M. d., Adriaenssens, S. & Adams, R. P. (2024).** Real-time design of architectural structures with differentiable mechanics and neural networks. arXiv. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02606
   This paper pairs a differentiable mechanics simulator with neural networks so structures can be designed in real time. The arXiv record lists no journal or conference version.

Related work that doesn't use AD: Bleker, Pastrana, Ohlbrock & D'Acunto (2022), *Structural Form-Finding Enhanced by Graph Neural Networks*, https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-13249-0_3.

All of this comes from OpenAlex, Crossref and arXiv, which may not list his most recent papers. His Google Scholar page or the jax_fdm GitHub repository would show anything newer.
