Yes. The most direct match is a 2026 paper from Princeton's Form-Finding Lab (Adriaenssens, Adams). I checked every reference below against Crossref or arXiv records.

**Closest match**

- Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Bletzinger, K.-U., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2026). *Differentiable force density method for the design of lightweight structures.* Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering 458, 118783. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cma.2026.118783
  - It combines automatic differentiation with analytical adjoints for the force density method on pin-jointed bar networks. One of its applications is planarizing gridshell panels while also minimizing support reactions. Membranes and masonry vault retrofits are the others.
  - I only saw the abstract and a search summary, not the full text. Check the paper before you rely on how much gridshell detail it has.

**Earlier work it builds on**

- Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* ICML 2023 Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407. This is the open-source library ([PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/jax-fdm/0.6.0)).
- Pastrana, R., Ohlbrock, P. O., Oberbichler, T., D'Acunto, P. & Parascho, S. (2023). *Constrained Form-Finding of Tension–Compression Structures using Automatic Differentiation.* Computer-Aided Design 155, 103435. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2022.103435. Note that this uses the Combinatorial Equilibrium Modeling method, not the force density method. Don't merge it with the JAX FDM paper; they're often confused.
- Pastrana, R., Medina, E., Oliveira, I. M. d., Adriaenssens, S. & Adams, R. P. (2024). *Real-time design of architectural structures with differentiable mechanics and neural networks.* https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.02606

**Related but not FDM**

- Wu, G. (2023). *A framework for structural shape optimization based on automatic differentiation, the adjoint method and accelerated linear algebra.* Struct. Multidisc. Optim. 66, 151. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-023-03601-0. Same research group, but it's shape optimization rather than force density.

One search did not turn up any paper that compares differentiable force density approaches on gridshells specifically. There may be more in IASS proceedings, for example [this 2023 one](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/contentone/iass/piass/2023/00002023/00000003/art00018), which I didn't open.
