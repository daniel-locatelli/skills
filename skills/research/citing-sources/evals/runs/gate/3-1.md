Here are five key references on planar quad (PQ) meshes for architectural freeform surfaces, in chronological order. I checked each DOI against its Crossref record and copied the details from there.

1. **Glymph, J., Shelden, D., Ceccato, C., Mussel, J. & Schober, H. (2004).** A parametric strategy for free-form glass structures using quadrilateral planar facets. *Automation in Construction* 13, 187–202. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autcon.2003.09.008
   The practice-side starting point, from Gehry Technologies and Schlaich Bergermann. It builds glazed freeform roofs from surfaces of translation and scale-trans surfaces, which give flat quad glass panels by construction.

2. **Liu, Y., Pottmann, H., Wallner, J., Yang, Y. L. & Wang, W. (2006).** Geometric modeling with conical meshes and developable surfaces. *ACM Transactions on Graphics* 25, 681–689. https://doi.org/10.1145/1141911.1141941
   The core PQ paper. It shows that PQ meshes discretize conjugate curve networks, and gives an optimization with subdivision that makes faces planar. It also introduces conical meshes, which allow offsets at constant face distance and so give clean multi-layer node geometry.

3. **Zadravec, M., Schiftner, A. & Wallner, J. (2010).** Designing Quad-dominant Meshes with Planar Faces. *Computer Graphics Forum* 29, 1671–1679. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2010.01776.x
   Lays out planar-face meshes from conjugate direction fields, guided by design intent such as alignment to features, boundaries or principal directions.

4. **Liu, Y., Xu, W., Wang, J., Zhu, L., Guo, B., Chen, F. & Wang, G. (2011).** General planar quadrilateral mesh design using conjugate direction field. *ACM Transactions on Graphics* 30, 1–10. https://doi.org/10.1145/2070781.2024174
   Extends conjugate-field PQ design to general surfaces, including singularities and regions of negative curvature, and turns the field into an actual PQ mesh.

5. **Pottmann, H., Eigensatz, M., Vaxman, A. & Wallner, J. (2015).** Architectural geometry. *Computers & Graphics* 47, 145–164. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cag.2014.11.002
   A survey of the field. It places PQ meshes alongside other paneling, support-structure and statics-aware methods, and its bibliography is a good way into the wider literature.

Two of the DOIs I first remembered, for Glymph et al. and Zadravec et al., pointed to unrelated papers. The DOIs above are the corrected ones.

Related papers I didn't look up in detail:
- **Pottmann et al. (2007)**, "Geometry of multi-layer freeform structures for architecture", [ACM ToG](https://doi.org/10.1145/1276377.1276458).
- **Eigensatz et al. (2010)**, "Paneling architectural freeform surfaces", [ACM ToG](https://doi.org/10.1145/1778765.1778782). It deals with general paneling and cost, not PQ specifically.
