Here are five key references on planar quad meshes for architectural freeform surfaces, from earliest to latest:

1. **Glymph, J., Shelden, D., Ceccato, C., Mussel, J. & Schober, H. (2004).** A parametric strategy for free-form glass structures using quadrilateral planar facets. *Automation in Construction* 13, 187–202. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.autcon.2003.09.008
   *This comes from practice (Gehry Partners and Schlaich Bergermann). It builds planar-quad glass facades with translational and scale-trans surfaces, and it is the usual starting point for the problem.*

2. **Liu, Y., Pottmann, H., Wallner, J., Yang, Y. L. & Wang, W. (2006).** Geometric modeling with conical meshes and developable surfaces. *ACM Transactions on Graphics* 25, 681–689. https://doi.org/10.1145/1141911.1141941
   *This is the foundational paper on planar quad (PQ) meshes. It links them to conjugate curve networks, gives the optimisation that makes faces planar, and introduces conical meshes, which have clean face offsets.*

3. **Zadravec, M., Schiftner, A. & Wallner, J. (2010).** Designing Quad‐dominant Meshes with Planar Faces. *Computer Graphics Forum* 29, 1671–1679. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-8659.2010.01776.x
   *Fits a conjugate direction field to a surface to get PQ-dominant layouts with irregular vertices. This gives more design freedom than following principal curvature lines.*

4. **Liu, Y., Xu, W., Wang, J., Zhu, L., Guo, B., Chen, F. & Wang, G. (2011).** General planar quadrilateral mesh design using conjugate direction field. *ACM Transactions on Graphics* 30, 1–10. https://doi.org/10.1145/2070781.2024174
   *A general method for PQ meshes on arbitrary freeform surfaces. It optimises a conjugate direction field that the user can control, and it handles singularities.*

5. **Pottmann, H., Eigensatz, M., Vaxman, A. & Wallner, J. (2015).** Architectural geometry. *Computers & Graphics* 47, 145–164. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cag.2014.11.002
   *A survey of the field. It covers PQ meshes, offsets, support structures, paneling and the links to statics. Its bibliography is a good place to look for further reading.*

A useful extra: Pottmann, Liu, Wallner, Bobenko & Wang (2007), *Geometry of multi-layer freeform structures for architecture*, *ACM TOG* 26, 65, https://doi.org/10.1145/1276377.1276458. It covers PQ meshes with offsets and node geometry without torsion.

All six were looked up through Crossref, and the details above are copied from those records. That check mattered: the DOIs I first recalled for Glymph et al. and Zadravec et al. belonged to different papers, and the ones listed here are corrected. The 2006 Liu et al. paper is also indexed under a second DOI for the SIGGRAPH proceedings (10.1145/1179352.1141941); I've given the *ACM TOG* journal version.
