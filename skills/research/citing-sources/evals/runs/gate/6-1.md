**JAX FDM itself:** Pastrana, R., Oktay, D., Adams, R. P. & Adriaenssens, S. (2023). *JAX FDM: A differentiable solver for inverse form-finding.* Differentiable Almost Everything Workshop at the 40th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12407

From the arXiv abstract:
- **What it is:** an open-source solver that can be differentiated, used to design efficient 3D shapes such as domes, cable nets and towers. It solves the inverse problem: you set a target, such as a shape or force limits, and it finds the parameters that produce it.
- **How it works:** it combines the force density method with gradient-based optimisation and handles sparse matrices in a way that still allows gradients to be taken. Because it is written in JAX, it can be combined with other JAX libraries, for example to link form-finding with neural networks.
- **Demonstration:** two design examples.
- **Code:** https://github.com/arpastrana/jax_fdm
